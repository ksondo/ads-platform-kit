1. Document Purpose
This Business Requirements Specification defines the business scope, actors, journeys, rules,
information requirements, controls, intelligence requirements and phased capabilities for ADS
VentureWorks. Version 2.0 materially expands the platform with a Venture Progress & Evidence
Engine so that every venture can show not only who has joined, but what is being done, what has
been verified, what is blocked, how fast the venture is moving and how ready it is for launch,
revenue, financing and scale.

The BRS treats VentureWorks as a standalone platform connected to the wider Africa Digital Synergy
ecosystem. It does not assume that every contributor is a volunteer, employee, shareholder or
contractor. The platform must explicitly model the agreed relationship and preserve legal,
governance, privacy and financing boundaries.

2. Business Objectives
1. Enable startups and emerging organizations to expose their structure, maturity, capability gaps
   and open contribution needs.
2. Enable people and partner organizations to discover ventures where their skills, services,
   networks, resources or capital can contribute.
3. Provide a controlled staff/contributor pipeline that can support ADS itself and participating
   entrepreneurs.
4. Measure and validate contributions using platform evidence and connected systems where
   possible.
5. Measure venture progress through objectives, milestones, workstreams, activities, deliverables,
   evidence, validation and velocity.
6. Give each founder a live project-management workspace and an automatically generated venture
   progress view.
7. Publish controlled public progress information so prospective contributors and partners can
   distinguish active ventures from dormant ones.
8. Create financing-readiness evidence that authorized banks, MFIs, investors and development
   partners can use as an additional decision-support input.
9. Preserve a contribution history that supports later employment, compensation, commission,
   recognition or equity review according to the governing agreement.
10. Create reusable organization blueprints for ADS business opportunities so entrepreneurs
   understand the capabilities required to establish and operate a business.
11. Connect organization-building to ADS Software Factory, education, digitalization, finance and
   market-development services.
12. Provide ADS with a portfolio command centre capable of monitoring thousands and eventually
   100,000 organizations without manually chasing each entrepreneur.

3. Scope
3.1 In Scope
     Universal contributor and organization registration; venture/startup profiles and governed
      knowledge bases.
     Business-opportunity selection, organization blueprints, maturity-stage assessment and
      capability-gap analysis.
     Open roles, professional needs, contributor capability profiles, search, discovery and intelligent
      matching.
     Contribution agreements, onboarding, work assignment, evidence capture, validation and
      contribution ledger.
     Venture objectives, milestones, workstreams, activities, tasks, deliverables, dependencies,
      blockers and progress status.
     Project-owner dashboard, team dashboard, public progress profile and ADS portfolio command
      centre.
     Venture health, progress, velocity, execution reliability, evidence confidence and readiness
      indicators.
     External evidence connectors including GitHub and future CRM, accounting, document, payment
      and operational systems.
     Financing-readiness profile and consented financier view; bank/investor decision remains
      external.
     Board, adviser and governance workspace; proposed equity workflow and cap-table support
      subject to legal confirmation.
     RAG/AI assistance grounded in venture, project, blueprint and evidence knowledge.
     Integration with Software Factory, Education, Opportunity Intelligence and approved
      financial/technology partners.

3.2 Out of Scope for Initial Release
     Automatic legal issuance of shares or securities.
     Automatic conversion of contributors into employees.
     Automatic approval of loans, investments or credit.
     Automatic legal advice or incorporation without qualified professionals and required authorities.
     Custody of investment funds unless later provided through an appropriately licensed partner
      structure.
     Guaranteeing startup success, future employment, investment return or beneficiary employment.
     Treating a VentureWorks readiness score as a regulated credit score or substitute for lender
      underwriting.

8. Venture Progress & Evidence Engine - Major Expansion
This domain is a core differentiator of VentureWorks. The system shall distinguish team formation
from actual venture advancement. A role being filled is not evidence that work is progressing.
Progress is established through defined work, accepted deliverables and verifiable evidence.

         Foundational rule: ROLE FILLED != WORK COMPLETED. VentureWorks measures
         execution, not attendance.

8.1 Universal Execution Hierarchy
Every venture shall be capable of being represented using the following hierarchy:

        Venture
        Strategic objectives
        Milestones
        Workstreams
        Activities
        Tasks
        Deliverables
        Evidence
        Validation / acceptance
Organization structure and execution structure remain connected but distinct: the organigram
defines who should be responsible; the execution hierarchy records what must be done and what
was actually completed.

8.5 Public Venture Progress Profile
The public view is designed to create trust and recruitment value without exposing sensitive internal
information. A venture may publish its stage, active status, selected progress indicators, recent
verified activity, completed public milestones, current priorities, next milestone and open
contribution opportunities.

The public profile must never imply that a manually posted statement is independently verified
unless evidence has actually been validated.
