1. Executive Summary
Africa Digital Synergy requires a hybrid education and capability-development platform combining facilitated 
learning, self-paced education, Agentic assistance, continuous assessment, practical work and optional 
ecosystem collaboration. Version 2.0 aligns education with the eight Phase 1 participation pathways defined in 
ADS-BRS-01 while recognizing that not every pathway is a certification program.
The platform shall minimize unnecessary Agentic RAG consumption by delivering ordinary lessons, readings, 
fixed exercises and suitable assessments through conventional content delivery and offline-capable learning. 
Agentic resources are used where personalized explanation, analysis, tutoring, project assistance, adaptive 
assessment or grounded knowledge retrieval adds value.
Education is designed to move participants toward capability and participation: developers toward learning or 
Software Factory, entrepreneurs toward business creation, organizations toward transformation, IT 
departments toward stronger delivery capability, and qualified institutions toward education/certification 
partnerships.
2. Education Principles
 Education -> capability -> participation; certification is used only where proving competency has value.
 Self-paced means flexible learning velocity, not self-certification.
 Facilitated learning, continuous assessment and practical activities remain available where required.
 Agentic learning shall be governed by entitlements and shall not function as an unrestricted generic chatbot.
 Static/offline learning shall be preferred when intelligence is not needed.
 Sector examples and specialization sit under the participant's pathway rather than becoming excessive top-level
programs.
 External accreditation or partner certification shall never be claimed without formal authorization.

5. Common Foundation - Required Content Direction
5.1 Course 101 emphasis
Course 101 shall explain why digitalization exists and the socioeconomic problems it can solve before moving 
into technology. It shall include access barriers, service-delivery limitations, 
financial/digital/educational/health/economic/geographical inclusion, the progression Manual -> Digitized -> 
Digitalized -> Connected -> Intelligent, and African examples such as mobile money as a mass-inclusion model.
The course shall connect socioeconomic problem -> need for access -> digitalization -> inclusion -> mass 
participation -> connected services -> data/knowledge/APIs -> AI/intelligent systems -> new services, businesses 
and markets.
5.2 Role-specific 200-level directions
 Developer: software development for digitalization, APIs/data/cloud, Software Factory, AI application 
development, RAG/Agentic systems, security/testing/deployment and practical project work.
 Entrepreneur: digital entrepreneurship, problem/opportunity discovery, business models, validation, financial 
projections, digital operations, regulation, financing readiness, launch and commercialization.
 Business digitalization: process mapping, digital maturity, customer access, payments, data, APIs, automation, 
AI, cybersecurity and transformation roadmap.
 IT Department: architecture, APIs, integration, modernization, Software Factory, data, automation, AI/Agentic 
RAG, security, governance and deployment.
 Intelligent Solutions: executive understanding of AI, Agentic AI, RAG, automation, knowledge readiness, 
governance, risk, use cases, ROI and POC decisions.
6. Enrollment, Identity and Entitlements
An ADS account is distinct from an education subscription. Registration creates identity; enrollment grants 
program-specific education entitlements. A person may hold multiple roles and may later move from student to 
developer, entrepreneur, organization member or project participant without creating a new account.
 Programs, modules, Agentic allowances, assessments, collaboration and certificates shall be entitlement 
controlled.
 Organizational subscriptions may enroll approved staff.

7. Controlled Agentic Learning and Cost Governance
Agentic RAG usage is a metered platform resource. The education platform shall use it deliberately and shall not
allow learners or institutions to consume unrestricted units simply because content is educational.
 Ordinary lesson text, readings, diagrams, downloadable materials, fixed quizzes and progress tracking should 
not invoke Agentic RAG unless required.
 Agentic usage is appropriate for personalized explanations, grounded Q&A, adaptive examples, tutoring, project
analysis, remediation, scenario analysis and selected assessments.
 Each enrolled program/package may carry a configurable AI allowance or credit budget.
 Usage shall be attributable to learner, organization, program, course and entitlement.
 Warnings and upgrade/renewal paths shall be available as allowances approach exhaustion.
 Partner or organizational access may include larger allowances but shall not default to unlimited usage.
 Administrators shall be able to change allowances without code changes.
 Internal metering should reflect real consumption even if the user sees simplified credits/interactions.
8. Facilitated Learning
 Physical training and bootcamps.
 Live webinars and expert-led sessions.
 Workshops and practical labs.
 University/partner-hosted sessions and regional centres.
 Attendance and participation evidence.
 Human-led assessments where required.
 Recordings/materials where rights permit.
9. Self-Paced Progression and Continuous Assessment
Learners may progress at different speeds. Progression shall depend on configured prerequisites, assessments, 
practical activities, interactive sessions and competency gates rather than an arbitrary universal completion 
period.
 Knowledge checks and structured exercises.
 Interactive Q&A and scenario analysis.
 Architecture/design exercises for technical pathways.
 Practical labs and implementation tasks.
 Projects and proof-of-concept activities.
 Milestone assessments and human-reviewed submissions.
 Assessment evidence retained according to program, privacy, certification and institutional rules.
10. Readiness Intelligence and Human Session Planning
The platform shall aggregate learners who are qualified or awaiting a particular facilitated activity and present 
this to authorized ADS administration/market operations. Readiness shall not automatically create a session. 
Authorized humans decide whether, where and when to organize onsite, regional or webinar activities.

11. Offline-First and Regional Access
To reduce connectivity barriers and Agentic consumption, suitable courses shall support downloadable learning
packs. Learners may study approved content, complete eligible exercises/quizzes and track progress offline, 
then synchronize when connectivity returns.
 Downloadable lessons/readings and permitted media.
 Offline exercises and eligible fixed assessments.
 Local progress state with later synchronization.
 Clear indication of activities that require online Agentic or human interaction.
 Conflict-safe synchronization and integrity controls.
 Design for variable connectivity and low-bandwidth conditions.
12. External Content Ingestion, Licensing and Provenance
The platform may ingest open educational resources or partner-provided materials only when the applicable 
rights permit the intended use. Free online access alone shall not be treated as permission to copy, adapt, 
redistribute or distribute offline.
Required Metadata Purpose
source_title / source_owner / source_url Identify origin and attribution.
license Record governing rights.
commercial_use_allowed Prevent incompatible commercial reuse.
adaptation_allowed Control modification/translation.
redistribution_allowed Control republishing.
offline_distribution_allowed Control downloadable packs.
attribution_required Ensure required credit.
original_version / date_retrieved Version and provenance tracking.
Publication shall be blocked or referred for review where rights are uncertain. For commercial ADS offerings, 
genuinely open resources with compatible rights, ADS original material and explicit institutional permissions 
should be prioritized.
13. Certification and Completion Governance
Certification shall not be the default outcome for every pathway. The platform shall distinguish attendance, 
completion, competency qualification, ADS certification and external/co-certification.
 Developer and selected IT Department pathways may support strong competency certification.
 Entrepreneur programs may provide completion or business-readiness recognition where useful.
 Digitalize My Business normally produces assessment/roadmap outcomes rather than professional certification.
 Explore Intelligent Solutions normally produces learning/engagement outcomes rather than certification.
 Education, Financial and Technology Partner pathways are not learner certification programs.
 University or external certification/co-certification requires a formal agreement and applicable authorization.
 Certificates shall be verifiable and linked to the applicable assessment/authorization basis.

15. Developer Education-to-Software-Factory Journey
The Developer pathway shall deliberately connect learning to productive participation. A registered developer 
may first experience a controlled demonstration of project analysis/specification/design assistance. The 
participant may then choose an education/student package or a paid Developer/Software Factory subscription.
Education shall prepare the developer for Software Factory participation but shall not duplicate the detailed 
build, deployment, hosting, Digital Haven or continuity requirements of ADS-BRS-03.
16. Entrepreneur Education-to-Business Journey
Entrepreneur learning shall support the journey Training -> Opportunity/Idea -> Digital Tools -> Business 
Planning -> Financial Projections -> Market Preparation -> Financing where necessary -> Launch -> Growth. 
Financing shall be treated as a possible enabler after business qualification, not as an automatic ADS product or 
promise.
17. Business and IT Department Education Journeys
Digitalize My Business shall provide enough learning for owners/managers to understand their digital maturity 
and transformation choices without requiring them to become technologists. Empower Our IT Department shall
provide deeper professional learning and may lead into Software Factory, modernization, integration, 
automation and intelligent systems.
ADS shall position IT enablement as strengthening internal teams rather than replacing them.
18. Explore Intelligent Solutions Journey
Executives and solution-aware participants shall receive concise, decision-oriented education. The intended 
flow is Education -> Opportunity Discovery -> Use Case -> Assessment -> POC -> Commercial Project where 
appropriate. Mature buyers shall not be forced through introductory coursework.
19. Collaboration & Innovation Network
Collaboration remains a shared ADS Member Ecosystem capability rather than an Education-only social 
network. Participation is opt-in, discoverability is private by default, and permission-aware Agentic matching 
may recommend people, projects, communities or opportunities without exposing restricted information.
 Consent-based profiles and messaging.
 Sector communities and moderated discussion rooms.
 Idea/project discussions with IP/confidentiality notices.
 Find-a-collaborator and team formation.
 Research/university and entrepreneur networking.
 Project/opportunity matching where authorized.
 Block/report and anti-spam controls.

25. Implementation Principle
The first implementation architecture shall anticipate focused pathways, offline/static learning, controlled 
Agentic usage, continuous assessment, human-controlled sessions, governed certification, content-rights 
management and cross-platform entitlements from inception. The platform shall not assume that every 
registered user is a paid learner or that every learner receives unlimited Agentic access.
