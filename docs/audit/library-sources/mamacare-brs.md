1. What MamaCare Intelligence Is
MamaCare Intelligence is Africa Digital Synergy's maternal, pregnancy and newborn platform. It
develops the earlier Pregnant Women's Avenue concept into a complete mother-and-child continuum
covering pregnancy preparation, pregnancy, antenatal care, labour and delivery, postpartum recovery,
newborn care and the infant's first year.
The platform is intended to serve both families and professionals. Mothers receive understandable,
personalized education and care navigation; midwives and clinicians receive structured follow-up tools;
authorized facilities can coordinate appointments and referrals; and the intelligence layer retrieves from
approved maternal and newborn health knowledge rather than treating a general AI model as the
clinical authority.

 Complete care journey
 Preconception -> Pregnancy -> Antenatal Care -> High-Risk Pathway -> Labour & Delivery ->
 Postpartum Mother -> Newborn -> Infant -> Continuing Mother & Child Follow-up




2. The Problem the Platform Addresses
Maternal and infant outcomes are influenced by much more than what happens in the delivery room.
Risk can begin before pregnancy; warning signs can emerge between appointments; transport and
referral delays can turn a manageable complication into an emergency; and support often drops sharply
after the baby is born.
   Women may enter pregnancy without understanding how previous pregnancy complications or
    chronic conditions can affect the next pregnancy.
   Antenatal information, blood-pressure readings, laboratory results, appointments, nutrition advice
    and emergency plans can remain fragmented.
   Danger signs may be recognized late or interpreted as normal pregnancy discomfort.
   High-risk mothers need closer follow-up, but missed appointments and disconnected records can
    make continuity difficult.
   Families may not know where to go, whom to call or how to arrange transport when labour or
    complications begin.
   Postpartum monitoring can receive less attention even though the mother's health remains important
    after delivery.
   Newborn danger signs can progress rapidly, while caregivers may delay seeking care because they
    are unsure what is serious.
   Health education is often generic rather than adapted to the mother's stage, history, care plan and
    local service environment.


3. Preconception and Pregnancy Preparation
MamaCare begins before pregnancy for women who are planning a pregnancy or who want to
understand their reproductive and maternal-health risks. The goal is earlier preparation and earlier
professional assessment where appropriate.

5. Pre-eclampsia Risk Awareness and Early-Warning Programme
Pre-eclampsia can become one of MamaCare's flagship maternal-safety pathways. The platform should
not claim that AI diagnoses or prevents pre-eclampsia. Its role is to support early risk awareness,
adherence to clinician-directed prevention and monitoring plans, recognition of warning signs and rapid
escalation.

 Risk-management journey
 Previous history and risk factors -> antenatal assessment -> blood-pressure monitoring -> symptoms
 and relevant clinical information -> clinician-defined prevention plan -> reminders -> warning-
 condition detection -> urgent professional assessment when required

Where a qualified clinician determines that a woman is at increased risk, the platform can support
adherence to the clinician's prevention plan. This may include clinician-prescribed measures such as low-
dose aspirin or calcium supplementation where appropriate, but MamaCare itself does not prescribe
them.
   Record or receive authorized blood-pressure readings from home, clinic or connected devices where
    available.
   Remind the mother about clinician-scheduled monitoring and follow-up.
   Explain why closer monitoring has been recommended using approved maternal-health knowledge.
   Educate about warning symptoms such as severe headache, visual disturbance, severe upper
    abdominal pain, breathing difficulty, concerning swelling or markedly elevated blood pressure.
   Escalate predefined warning conditions toward urgent professional assessment instead of attempting
    a home diagnosis.
   Continue postpartum blood-pressure and symptom follow-up for women whose care team requires it.

8. Postpartum Mother
The mother's journey does not end when the baby is delivered. MamaCare continues through the
postpartum period with education, follow-up and escalation appropriate to the mother's recovery.
   Postpartum bleeding and other clinician-approved danger-sign education.
   Blood-pressure follow-up, particularly where hypertension or pre-eclampsia was present or
    suspected.
   Infection awareness and follow-up.
   Caesarean or wound recovery guidance under the care team's instructions.
   Pain, anaemia, nutrition, hydration and recovery education.
   Breastfeeding support and referral to qualified assistance where needed.
   Sleep, exhaustion and practical support for the mother.
   Mental-health and postpartum-depression awareness with confidential referral pathways.
   Family-planning and contraception education linked to professional care.
   Postnatal appointments and return-to-activity guidance based on the care plan.

10. Infant Survival and Early Escalation
MamaCare cannot itself prevent infant mortality, but it can address an important chain that contributes
to avoidable harm: late recognition of danger, delayed decision-making, delayed transport and delayed
treatment.

 Early escalation model
 Caregiver notices concern -> guided screening questions -> predefined risk categorization -> no home
 diagnosis -> referral recommendation -> appropriate facility -> transport/ambulance connection ->
 caregiver instructions while seeking professional help


   Difficulty feeding or inability to feed.
   Fever or abnormal temperature.
   Breathing difficulty.
   Severe lethargy or unusual unresponsiveness.
   Convulsions.
   Concerning jaundice.
   Signs of dehydration.
   Other clinician-approved newborn or infant warning signs.
Emergency guidance must be conservative: when predefined danger conditions are present, the platform
should prioritize professional assessment rather than prolonged conversation with an AI assistant.

12. Maternal Nutrition Intelligence
Nutrition is central across preconception, pregnancy, postpartum recovery and infant feeding. MamaCare
can provide a maternal-specific nutrition experience while reusing the broader ADS Nutrition
Intelligence platform where appropriate.
For example, a pregnant woman who is anaemic, has a limited food budget and lives in Cameroon could
request a locally relevant meal plan. MamaCare can pass pregnancy stage, clinician-defined restrictions
and nutrition objectives to Nutrition Intelligence, which returns suitable options from approved nutrition
knowledge and deterministic dietary calculations.
   Pregnancy-stage nutrition education.
   Budget-aware local food planning.
   Clinician-defined dietary restrictions.
   Anaemia and other condition-specific nutrition support within professional guidance.
   Postpartum and breastfeeding nutrition.
   Infant feeding and age-appropriate nutrition education.

16. Business Model and Commercial Potential
MamaCare can be deployed as both a consumer platform and an institutional maternal-health service.
The strongest commercial model is likely to combine family access with hospitals, insurers, employers,
NGOs and public-health programmes.
   Individual or family subscriptions for premium pregnancy and newborn support features.
   Hospital, clinic and maternity-centre subscriptions for patient engagement and follow-up.
   Employer maternal-wellbeing packages.
   Insurance or financial-partner integrations.
   Implementation and integration services for healthcare networks.
   Maternal-health education and community programmes for NGOs and development organizations.
   Knowledge digitisation and governed AI services for hospitals or maternal-health programmes.
   Professional teleconsultation or counselling connections where licensed providers and local rules
    permit.
   API and integration services connecting laboratories, pharmacies, transport and other approved
    providers.
Commercial growth should not depend on exploiting sensitive health data. The platform's value comes
from service, continuity, trusted intelligence, integration and improved access to care.


17. Privacy, Clinical Governance and Safety
   Health and pregnancy information is sensitive and should be collected only for defined purposes with
    appropriate consent and access controls.
   A mother's private information should not automatically become visible to an employer, insurer,
    school, family member or unrelated provider.
   Clinical roles and permissions should determine which professional can see which information.
   The AI assistant must not present itself as a doctor, independently prescribe medicines or override
    clinician instructions.
   High-risk and emergency pathways should use approved clinical rules and conservative escalation
    rather than generative-model judgment alone.
   Knowledge sources should be professionally reviewed, versioned and traceable.
   Local medical, privacy, telemedicine, child-protection and health-data requirements must be
    addressed before production deployment in each market.

19. Strategic Business Potential
MamaCare's long-term opportunity is larger than pregnancy content. It can become a maternal and child-
health relationship that begins before conception and continues through the baby's first year and beyond.
Every stage creates a legitimate service need: education, appointments, monitoring, nutrition, laboratory
services, pharmacy, transport, insurance, professional consultation and child-health follow-up.
For healthcare providers, it creates continuity between visits. For mothers, it reduces fragmentation. For
partners, it creates an organized channel through which services can be delivered at the right stage. For
Africa Digital Synergy, it demonstrates how a vertical platform can combine digital operations, governed
knowledge, Agentic assistance and connections to other sector services without allowing AI to replace
professional responsibility.

 MamaCare proposition
 A continuous digital and intelligent care companion supporting women from pregnancy preparation
 through antenatal care, safe delivery, postpartum recovery and the infant's first year - connecting
 education, risk awareness, professional care, nutrition, emergency response and newborn
 development within one governed maternal-health ecosystem.




20. Closing Perspective
The original Pregnant Women's Avenue was an education centre for women during pregnancy.
MamaCare Intelligence expands that vision into an end-to-end maternal and newborn platform.
The central opportunity is continuity: the woman should not have to begin again with a different
disconnected system at preconception, antenatal care, delivery, postpartum recovery and newborn care.
MamaCare keeps those stages connected while ensuring that clinical decisions remain with qualified
professionals and that urgent situations are escalated toward real care.
