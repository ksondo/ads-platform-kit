1. Executive Summary
Africa Digital Synergy (ADS) requires a public digital platform that presents the vision of a connected and 
intelligent digital Africa while converting discovery into structured participation. The platform combines a 
complete conventional website with an optional Agentic Advisor that can independently guide a visitor from 
first contact through understanding, qualification, registration and the appropriate next action.
Version 2.0 narrows Phase 1 acquisition to eight primary participation pathways and introduces controlled 
Agentic usage. Anonymous visitors receive a small configurable demonstration allowance; registration creates a
shared ADS identity and unlocks role-appropriate experiences. The objective is not to provide unrestricted free 
AI access, but to demonstrate practical value and move qualified users into education, Software Factory, 
business digitalization, enterprise, or partner relationships.
The website remains a market-discovery and ecosystem-acquisition layer. Detailed education delivery belongs 
to ADS-BRS-02, while detailed Software Factory, Digital Haven, application continuity, deployment and hosting 
requirements belong to ADS-BRS-03.
2. Business Vision and Objectives
Working destination statement: A digitally connected and intelligent Africa where organizations, industries and 
essential services can interact across interoperable digital ecosystems, enabling intelligent technologies to serve 
people, businesses and institutions at scale.
 Present the destination first and reveal mechanisms progressively.
 Make discovery useful without requiring AI, while making the Agentic Advisor available from the beginning.
 Use Agentic interaction as a controlled demonstration, qualification and conversion mechanism rather than an 
unrestricted public chatbot.
 Focus Phase 1 on eight approved participation pathways.
 Create one ADS identity that can carry multiple roles, organizations, subscriptions and entitlements.
 Preserve sector context underneath the participation pathways instead of creating excessive top-level journeys.
 Route qualified demand to the correct ADS team, program, Software Factory environment or approved partner 
relationship.
 Measure acquisition, registration, conversion, Agentic consumption and commercial outcomes.

4. Sector Context Under the Pathways
Banking, insurance, healthcare, education, agriculture, retail, transport, logistics, manufacturing, 
telecommunications, tourism, real estate, software/IT, employment, nutrition, environment, government and 
other sectors remain important, but they are not separate Phase 1 acquisition doors.
Examples: a construction-company owner enters Digitalize My Business and then selects Construction; a bank 
IT director enters Empower Our IT Department and then selects Banking; a developer enters Join as Developer 
and later selects a sector or project domain.
5. Public Discovery and Agentic Advisor
5.1 Website-first and Advisor-first journeys
The conventional website shall remain complete without AI. The Agentic Advisor shall also be capable of zero￾context discovery from the first public entry point and context-preserving continuation from any sector, project,
education or partnership page.
5.2 Anonymous Agentic demonstration allowance
 Anonymous browsing of ordinary public website content shall not consume Agentic allowance.
 Anonymous Agentic interaction shall start with a configurable allowance of approximately five interactions.
 The value shall be administratively configurable and shall not be hard-coded.
 The Advisor shall use the small allowance to understand intent, answer high-value introductory questions and 
demonstrate relevance.
 When the allowance is exhausted or sufficient intent is established, the visitor shall be invited to register rather 
than continue unrestricted AI consumption.
 Anti-abuse controls shall reduce repeated anonymous resets and automated consumption.
5.3 Registration and qualification
Registration indicates stronger intent but does not itself create a paid subscription. The platform shall capture 
only the information needed to establish role, organization/department where applicable, sector, objective and 
relevant participation pathway.
A registered user may receive a larger configurable demonstration entitlement based on role and pathway. For 
example, a developer may receive a controlled project-analysis experience, while an IT department may receive
a controlled organizational sandbox or proof-of-value experience.

7. Shared Identity, Entitlements and Agentic Usage
One ADS identity shall be used across Website, Education, Software Factory, Projects, Collaboration and 
Partnerships. Access shall be controlled through roles, organization relationships, subscriptions and 
entitlements.
 Entitlements may be assigned by account, role, organization, department, program, subscription, project or 
partnership.
 Every Agentic interaction shall be attributable to an approved entitlement, allowance, subscription, program, 
project or internal purpose.
 Partners may receive larger allowances as part of an agreement, but partner access shall not be assumed 
unlimited.
 Administrators shall be able to configure allowances without code changes.
 Internal metering shall measure actual consumption, including model/retrieval/tool usage where available; 
customer-facing presentation may use simplified ADS AI Credits or equivalent units.
 Usage warnings, exhaustion states, upgrades and routing shall be configurable.
 Consumption analytics shall support cost per user, pathway, organization, program, partner and campaign.

9. Education Integration
Education shall be available where understanding or capability development is needed, but shall not be forced 
on mature commercial prospects. The website shall preserve role, sector, questions and qualification context 
when handing a participant to ADS-BRS-02.
Not all eight pathways are certification programs. Certification is reserved for pathways and competencies 
where formal proof adds value. Partner pathways are primarily onboarding and relationship journeys.
10. Software Factory and Digital Haven Integration
Developers and authorized organizational IT users shall be able to move from discovery or demonstration into 
the Software Factory using the same ADS identity. The handoff shall preserve project, sector, organization and 
entitlement context.
Detailed requirements for project analysis, specification assistance, code/repository integration, build lifecycle, 
deployment, hosting, security, observability, application knowledge, operations continuity and staff onboarding 
are defined in ADS-BRS-03 and shall not be duplicated here.
11. Partnerships
 Education/Certification Partner: institutional education, assessment, certification/co-certification, venue, 
research or lab relationships subject to formal authorization.
 Financial Partner: financing and financial-service relationships for qualified entrepreneurs, SMEs, projects or 
other approved ecosystem opportunities; financing is not automatically an ADS product.
 Technology Partner: underlying or complementary technologies may be integrated rather than rebuilt where 
partnership is strategically and technically appropriate.
 ADS shall not imply that a strategic technology partner participates in every opportunity; joint engagement 
occurs only when appropriate and authorized.
12. Marketing, CRM and Analytics
 Capture campaign/source, pathway, role, sector, organization/department, consent, qualification stage and 
outcome.
 Track anonymous-to-registration conversion and registration-to-paid/partner conversion.
 Track Agentic usage by anonymous, registered, subscribed and partner states.
 Track pathway-specific conversion into Education, Software Factory, digitalization projects, POCs and 
partnerships.
 Support configurable routing and ownership without dependency on a single CRM vendor.
 Support dashboards for Developer/Student, Entrepreneur/SME and Organization/Enterprise market teams.
13. Security, Privacy and AI Governance
 Secure authentication, authorization, encryption, least privilege and audit logging.
 Separate public, member, organization, project and partner knowledge.
 Permission-aware retrieval shall prevent restricted knowledge from leaking through Agentic responses.
 Do not request sensitive source code, credentials or organizational data during a public demonstration unless 
an approved secure workflow exists.

15. High-Level Acceptance Baseline
1. A visitor can discover ADS and the eight Phase 1 pathways without using AI.
2. An anonymous visitor can use the Agentic Advisor within a configurable small demonstration allowance, 
initially about five interactions.
3. Anonymous allowance exhaustion leads to registration/next-action guidance rather than unrestricted 
continued consumption.
4. Registration creates one ADS identity and can unlock role-appropriate demonstration entitlements.
5. A developer can be routed from demonstration toward Education or paid Software Factory participation.
6. An organization with an IT department can be routed toward IT enablement and Software Factory rather 
than being treated only as a software-outsourcing customer.
7. Not all pathways are represented as certification programs.
8. Every Agentic interaction can be attributed to an entitlement or approved purpose and measured for cost 
governance.
9. Sector context is preserved underneath the eight pathways.
10. Qualified leads reach the correct ADS team with consented context.
11. Education and Software Factory handoffs preserve identity, sector, organization and qualification context.
12. Restricted organizational, project and partner knowledge remains permission controlled.

17. Implementation Principle
The initial architecture shall support the eight pathways, anonymous and registered Agentic entitlements, 
shared identity, role/organization context, conversion routing and cross-platform handoff from inception. 
Business-configurable allowances and routing rules shall be data-driven rather than hard-coded.
