Role, Interview & Recruitment Intelligence Platform
Role intelligence, candidate readiness, interviewer preparation and recruitment assessment support
Business Requirements Specification (BRS)
Version 1.0 | 8 September 2026


1. Executive Summary
The platform began as an interview-preparation concept and expands naturally into a role, competency and recruitment intelligence layer for candidates, employers, HR departments, recruitment firms and job-search platforms.
Its defensible asset is the Role Intelligence Knowledge Base and Knowledge Factory: what a role requires, what evidence demonstrates competence, how candidates prepare and what interviewers verify.
2. Business Problem
Candidates often prepare with generic questions, while interviewers may assess roles they do not deeply understand. Recruitment systems manage vacancies and CV workflows but rarely provide deep, trustworthy role intelligence.
Generic preparation
Weak role-specific competency understanding
Non-specialists assessing technical roles
Inconsistent panels
Weak requirement-to-evidence linkage
Messy job-description ingestion
Fragmented professional/jurisdictional knowledge
3. Vision
Create reusable Role & Competency Intelligence infrastructure powering candidate preparation, interviewer intelligence and recruitment assessment support while preserving candidate privacy and human employment decision authority.
4. Business Objectives
Model roles, competencies, credentials, evidence and assessment areas
Help candidates prepare for target roles
Help executives/HR/technical interviewers conduct informed interviews
Help recruitment firms/job platforms enrich vacancies and candidate services
Ingest unstructured vacancies
Maintain trustworthy jurisdiction-aware knowledge
Support low-touch panel setup
Separate private candidate preparation from employer data
Support evidence organization without autonomous hiring decisions
