## 25 October 2026 — opportunity-specific imagery (PR: opportunity-photo-curation)

This follow-up adds an explicit source-controlled **121-solution ID → photographic key** registry. Every one of the 21 Business/Institutional groups has distinct images within the group. The initial 13 Retail/Wholesale/Food and 11 Processing/Manufacturing cards no longer repeat one cover; each image is selected for the business activity. Sources include photographs from Cameroon and, for two processing examples, Nigeria. They are **illustrations only**, not photographs of actual ADS partners, product deployments, workforces or regulated services.

All new Wikimedia Commons photograph source pages were inspected for the artist and the stated licence. ### Serving and performance limits

This release continues to request width-limited Wikimedia Commons thumbnails and previously approved Unsplash editorial images from their hosting services. New images are not local WebP and have not yet passed a cross-network transfer-budget/per-image 100 KB audit. An unavailable provider image should be visually hidden by the existing portal error handler with its card background retained. This work does not enable a service, modify database seeds or assign any new verified demo URL.
